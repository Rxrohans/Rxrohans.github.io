About me

I work where clinical context meets AI systems.

My academics cover healthcare and its technology. A B.Pharm from DIPSAR gave me a foundation in pharmacy and regulation. I'm passionate about MedTech product development and healthcare AI, where clinical grounding and AI fluency both matter.
